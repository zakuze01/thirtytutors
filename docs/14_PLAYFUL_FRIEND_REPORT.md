# Preview 10 — concise situational humor in Friend mode

The user wants a humorous Vietnamese/English companion with the feel they enjoy
in Hala Rabbit. Its public store listing identifies the app but does not disclose
its private persona or establish its exact dialogue style. No proprietary prompt,
dialogue or assets are copied and no exact imitation/quality equivalence is claimed.
Reference: [official store listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.halatech.halarabbit).

Friend + Nhây hài now adds a compact conversational direction: react to meaning,
choose at most one situational comic turn, vary openings and stop naturally.
Avoid constant nickname/fen/praise/laughter/question suffixes and customer-service
filler. Prefer little imaginary scenes, playful exaggeration and callbacks only
to real prior turns. Seven original illustrative mini-dialogues show bilingual
timing, a response to an ordinary statement, requested correction, sadness and
an explicit stop-joking command. Examples are marked as non-factual/non-scripted.
The approved 28-item slang catalog remains in source; only two greeting/praise
examples are injected rather than sending the whole list in every prompt.

Existing short-response limits, correction opt-in, memory consent, uncertainty
and child-friendly constraints remain. Humor never targets the learner's mistakes,
identity/body/family/ability. Distress, serious disclosures, unclear speech or a
request to stop switch to calm direct responses. No requirement to make every
turn funny or to turn casual chat into a drill. Teacher and Gentle do not receive
the new Friend comedy examples. No TTS/provider/cost-routing change.

Policy revision is bumped to 7 so Gemini does not resume an older persona;
Groq/DeepSeek receive the new system instruction on their existing reply path.
App version is 1.2.1+companionlite.10. Offline eSpeak remains robotic: prompt
changes alter words, not neural prosody, laughter or voice acting.

No new literal-string tests are added for this reversible editorial change.
Existing policy/config/routing/provider regression checks validate assembly and
boundaries. They cannot prove jokes are funny or model instructions always obeyed.
No paid cloud evaluation is performed. User listening/subjective assessment remains
necessary after key entry and connection.

For manual listening, try ordinary topics (hungry cat, food, a lazy day), a correct
English statement, an explicit correction request, sadness, 'đừng đùa nữa', and
an unclear utterance. Assess: relevant rather than random joke; short reply;
correct usable English; no repeated stock catchphrase; no pressure to answer;
seriousness respected; no invented memories. Example lines in source are target
illustrations, not recorded model outputs.

Build and test evidence is appended after verification. No public release.

Pre-build verification: 94 existing policy/config/live-routing/Groq/DeepSeek
checks pass, zero failures/skips, 101.97s. Ruff lint/format and diff whitespace
